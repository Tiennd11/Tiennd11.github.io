Research interests

One question runs through all of it: how do you put physics inside a learning system so that it generalises, converges, and can be trusted on problems where data is expensive and the governing equations are already known?

Learning methods how the model is built

Physics-informed learning

Putting the governing equation in the loss. Physics-informed networks and neural operators for PDEs with discontinuities, multiple domains, and multi-scale structure.

Uncertainty quantification

Heteroscedastic Gaussian processes and ensembles for problems where the noise itself changes across the input space, not just the prediction.

Large language models

Transformer models and retrieval-augmented pipelines pointed at engineering literature and data, where an answer is only useful if it can be checked against its source.

Agentic AI for science

Systems that run their own loop — proposing a hypothesis space for sparse system discovery, testing it, and revising, instead of waiting to be handed one.

Perception and systems what it is pointed at

Computer vision

Reading images and video as measurements: microstructure images that predict material properties, and camera pipelines that return numbers rather than labels.

Robot learning and control

Inverse kinematics for redundant manipulators as set prediction, sparse regression for multibody dynamics, and disturbance-rejecting control for nonlinear arms.

Materials science

Nonlinear behaviour of nanocomposite plates and shells, and data-driven design that searches a composition space instead of enumerating it.

Structural health monitoring

Vibration signatures read as a diagnostic: what a shift in the mode shape says about where damage sits and how far it has grown.

Applied work

Where the research leaves the paper: systems that have to run on somebody else's site, on their hardware, against their data.

VCV CAM — analytics on cameras a site already owns

Tech lead · vcv-cam.onrender.com

An RTSP/ONVIF stream goes in, rule-based detection runs on-premises, and what comes out is an alert with a snapshot, a timestamp, and a searchable history. Operators draw their own zones on the camera frame, so a site is configured without code and without replacing hardware. Deployed across intrusion and crowd detection, early smoke and flame, protective-equipment checks, surface defect inspection and product counting, livestock counting, plate recognition and parking occupancy, and OCR for container codes.

VMV Technologies

Tech lead · vmvtechnologies.com

AI, software and robotics built for Vietnamese businesses on their own data and processes — consulting through integration, then handover of the source, so the client owns the system instead of renting it.

Image-based AI for material design

AI Research Intern · S2Labs, Oregon, 2025

Deep learning models that read microstructure images and predict material properties, closing the loop into data-driven design optimisation. Prepared the image datasets, trained the models, and ran the evaluation.
